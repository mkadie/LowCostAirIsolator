# Contributing

Thanks for looking at the Low-Cost Air Isolator. This is an **early, unfunded, open-source** assistive-technology prototype, and we genuinely want feedback — **from experts, makers, and users alike**. Critical, specific input is more useful to us than encouragement.

## Who we'd like to hear from

We'd love to hear from **anyone with relevant expertise**, from **other open-source assistive-device makers**, and — most of all — from **people who use assistive devices**. Every perspective makes this better, and small contributions add up. You don't need to be an expert to help: print reports, photos, and "this didn't work" notes are all genuinely useful.

## The questions we're stuck on

See the "We're looking for feedback" section of [`air-isolator-prototype-v1.md`](./air-isolator-prototype-v1.md). In short:

- Is the **screw-clamp seal** reliable and repeatable?

## How to contribute

- **Open an [Issue](https://github.com/mkadie/LowCostAirIsolator/issues)** for a question, bug, test result, or suggestion. Please say whether you're reporting from **bench testing**, **printing**, or **domain expertise**, and include photos, print settings, filament, and measurements where relevant.
- **Start a Discussion** for open-ended design or research ideas.
- **Pull requests** are welcome for STLs/CAD, docs, cost-model inputs, or test data. Keep changes focused and describe what you changed and why.
- **Email:** main@tssfaa.com if a public thread isn't appropriate.

## Ground rules

- **Safety first.** This touches vulnerable users and the mouth. Flag anything that could be a hygiene, choking, biocompatibility, or electrical hazard.
- **No medical/FDA claims.** Please don't add language asserting clearance, certification, or compliance. See [`DISCLAIMER.md`](./DISCLAIMER.md).
- **Cite sources.** Prefer peer-reviewed or primary sources; label datasheets, vendor pages, and anecdotes as such.
- **Be kind and specific.** Assume good faith; we're volunteers.
